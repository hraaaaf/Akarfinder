# Salé — Product Geography Evidence

Status: identity-only canonicalization batch. No product boundary promotion.

## Decision

Salé is expanded with five canonical map neighborhoods/territorial product identities:

- Bab Lamrissa
- Tabriquet
- Bettana
- Laayayda
- Hssaine

These names are supported by two independent evidence layers:

1. **Official planning / administrative evidence** — the Agence Urbaine de Rabat-Salé identifies the Salé PAU and the five arrondissements Bab Lamrissa, Tabriquet, Bettana, Laayayda and Hssaine. It also publishes homologated planning documents for the individual sectors.
2. **Modern real-estate product taxonomy** — Yakeey publicly exposes Salé neighborhood entries including Bab Lemrissa, Bettana, Tabriquet, Layayda and Hssain.
